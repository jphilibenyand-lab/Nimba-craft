Our story

Nimba Craft Corner began with a simple idea: everyday objects should be made well, made by hand, and made to last.

How we started

What began as a weekend hobby, weaving baskets for family and neighbours, grew as more people asked for pieces of their own. Soon the table in the corner of the house became a small workshop, and the workshop became a business.

We named it after the mountain that shaped our patterns, our materials and our way of working: patiently, and with respect for the land.

How we make

Each piece starts with natural fibres that we gather, sort and dye ourselves. We weave without machines, so every basket, bag and mat carries small differences that show it was made by a person.

A single item can take anywhere from a few hours to several days, depending on its size and pattern.

Every piece is made to order or in small batches, so nothing sits in a warehouse.

What we stand for

Made by hand

No shortcuts. Every strand is placed by a person who cares how it turns out.

Natural materials

We choose fibres and dyes that are kind to the people who use them and the place they come from.

Built to last

Our pieces are tightly woven and repairable, so you can keep them for years, not seasons.
